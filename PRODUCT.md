# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary:** café owner-managers of small operations (~5–50 people) who need to know whether opening checks actually happened, without digging through WhatsApp photo dumps. Context is busy, often pre-service or pre-inspection pressure; the job is trust and action on gaps.

**Secondary:** floor staff who start an opening shift check and upload 3–8 photos. They should finish proof as a normal habit, not a heroic extra task.

MVP tenancy is one demo café; roles are staff and manager only.

## Product Purpose

ShiftProof turns chaotic phone photos into structured shift proof: AI scores checklist items against café SOP clauses as Pass / Gap / Unclear, managers see a live gap scoreboard, can override and assign fixes, and can export a 1-page audit pack.

Success looks like staff routinely leaving photo proof at opening, and managers seeing trustworthy, clause-cited gaps fast enough to act—without free chat or enterprise QMS complexity.

## Positioning

Every screen reinforces the same claim: **opening proof is a staff habit that leaves managers clause-cited gaps they can trust—not chat threads they have to interpret.**

## Brand Personality

**Clear · trustworthy · calm.** Status language is decisive and plain. The product feels like a quiet ops tool under pressure: evidence first, no flash, no panic theater, no chatbot personality.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboards** — purple gradients, hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, “AI chat” chrome
- **Enterprise QMS / heavy ERP** — dense multi-column admin, certification theater, everything-is-a-table
- **Consumer social / WhatsApp clones** — chatty photo feeds and conversation UI standing in for structured findings

## Design Principles

1. **Manager sees truth first** — gaps, status, and next actions outrank decoration; the scoreboard is the hero, not the chrome.
2. **Proof is habitual, not heroic** — staff flows stay short, obvious, and phone-friendly so evidence becomes routine.
3. **Structure over chat** — Pass / Gap / Unclear plus clause and confidence; never free-form SOP chat.
4. **Calm under pressure** — clear hierarchy and status color with labels; no alarmist UI or celebration noise.
5. **Trust is earned by evidence** — every judgment is inspectable (clause, quote, confidence, audit events).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG certification bar for the hackathon MVP. Use sensible defaults: readable contrast, visible focus, labeled controls, and respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` where motion exists. Status must never rely on color alone.
